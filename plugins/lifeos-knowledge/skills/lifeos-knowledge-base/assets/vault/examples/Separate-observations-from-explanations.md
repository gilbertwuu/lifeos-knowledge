# Separate observations from explanations

> [!abstract] Summary
> Record what you can observe, state possible explanations separately, and choose a check that can tell them apart.

Imagine a plant with one yellow leaf. The color is an observation. Too little water, too much water, or ordinary leaf aging are possible explanations. Check the soil and the condition of other leaves before deciding how to respond.

| Observation | Possible explanation | Next check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One leaf is yellow | The soil may be too dry or too wet | Check soil moisture and whether other leaves are changing |

This method helps when the same observation can support several plausible explanations. The plant is an illustrative example.
